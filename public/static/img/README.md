# Branding assets

No approved National Data Center Somalia logo or brand assets were supplied.
`placeholder-mark.svg` and the text badge in the sidebar are **neutral placeholders**.

To apply approved branding:
1. Place the approved logo files in this folder (e.g. `ndc-logo.svg`).
2. Replace the `brand-mark` span in `resources/views/components/layout.blade.php`
   and `guest-layout.blade.php` with an `<img>` tag pointing to the file.
3. Adjust the colour tokens at the top of `public/assets/css/app.css`.

Do not use national emblems or government marks without written approval.
