# Staff user guide

## Signing in

Open the NDC-SMS address, enter your official email and password. After 5 wrong attempts the account is locked for 5 minutes. Forgot your password? Use *Forgot your password?* — a reset link is emailed if your account is active. You can switch language (EN / SO) at any time in the top bar.

Recommended: *My profile → Two-step verification* — install an authenticator app, enter the key shown, confirm with the 6-digit code and store the recovery codes safely.

## Dashboard

Shows live figures you are allowed to see: your open and overdue tasks, approvals waiting for you, upcoming meetings and deadlines, and — depending on your role — projects, documents, correspondence, procurement, assets and budget figures. Click a figure to open the matching filtered list. Empty boxes mean nothing has been registered yet.

## Tasks

* *Tasks → Assigned to me* lists your work; *Overdue only* filters late items.
* Open a task to update progress (0–100 %) and status, add comments and attach files.
* *Submit completion*: describe the evidence (and attach files). If the task needs approval it goes to the approver; you will be notified of approval or rejection (with a note).
* Managers create tasks, assign/reassign them and approve completions. You cannot approve your own task.

## Projects

Project pages show objectives, progress (calculated from completed tasks), milestones, risks and issues, stakeholders, tasks, budget summary, documents and history. Project managers edit their own projects; *Project report* gives a printable summary.

## Documents

* *Register document*: title, category, confidentiality and the file (PDF, Office, images, text, CSV — up to the configured size). The system checks that the file content matches its type.
* Confidentiality: **Public** and **Internal** — all staff with document access; **Confidential** — your department, the owner and people granted access; **Restricted** — only named people.
* Upload new versions with a change note; earlier versions remain available.
* *Submit for review* sends it to approvers; you cannot approve your own document.

## Correspondence

Registry officers record incoming and outgoing letters (with scans). Assigned officers can forward letters, add follow-up notes and change the status (e.g. *Responded*, *Closed*). Overdue responses are highlighted and reminded.

## Meetings & calendar

See upcoming meetings, the month calendar and past meetings. Organisers record minutes, attendance, decisions and action points; an action point can be turned into a task for the responsible person.

## Procurement

1. *New purchase request*: items, quantities and estimated unit costs (totals are calculated).
2. *Submit* — an approver (never you) approves or rejects.
3. The procurement officer records quotations and raises the purchase order; someone other than you and the approver records goods received.
You can always see the status of your own requests.

## Budgets & expenses (finance roles)

Budgets are split into category allocations and approved by someone other than the preparer. Expenses are recorded against an approved budget, approved by another person (never above the allocation), and later marked *Paid* and *Reconciled* — these only record what happened in the accounting system; NDC-SMS makes no payments.

## Reports

*Reports* lists the reports your role may run. Use filters, then *Export CSV* or *Export PDF* (if you have export permission). Exports are logged.

## Notifications

The bell shows unread notifications: new assignments, approvals, rejections, meeting invitations and reminders. Click one to open the related record.
