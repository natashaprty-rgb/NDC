# Staff User Guide

This guide explains how NDC staff use the Management System day to day. What
you see depends on your role: menu items and buttons you are not permitted to
use are hidden.

## Signing in

1. Open the system address in your browser and enter your official e-mail and
   password.
2. If two-factor authentication is enabled, enter the 6-digit code from your
   authenticator app (or one of your recovery codes).
3. The first time, you will be asked to choose a new password: at least 10
   characters with upper- and lower-case letters, a number and a symbol.

Forgot your password? Use **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page; a
reset link is e-mailed to you. After five wrong attempts you must wait a
minute.

**Language**: switch between **EN** (English) and **SO** (Soomaali) at the
top of every page. Your choice is remembered and also used for e-mails.

**Your account** (top-right menu): update your name, phone and language under
*My profile*; change your password, enable two-factor authentication, see
where you are signed in and sign out other devices under *Security &
sessions*.

## Dashboard

Your starting page: headline figures for the modules you can access, project
status and progress, overdue tasks, your own tasks, the next 14 days of
meetings and deadlines, budget position (finance roles), notifications and
recent activity. Every figure is calculated from current records. A button at
the top shows how many items are waiting for your approval.

## Notifications

The bell icon shows unread notifications: new assignments, approval requests
and decisions, reminders of deadlines, meetings, expiring documents and
warranties. Click one to open the related record. If e-mail notifications are
enabled you also receive them by e-mail.

## Tasks

- **My tasks**: *Tasks* lists tasks assigned to you, created by you or
  assigned to your department.
- **Updating**: open a task, set its progress and status (*In Progress*),
  add comments and upload evidence (files marked as completion evidence).
- **Finishing**: when the task requires review, click **Submit for review**;
  a reviewer approves it (it becomes *Completed*) or returns it with a
  comment (*Returned*) — then continue and submit again. Tasks that do not
  require review can be completed directly.
- **Managers** (create permission) create tasks, assign them to a person or a
  department, set priority, deadline and expected deliverable, and reassign.
  The assignee is notified.
- Overdue tasks are highlighted; reminders are sent before the deadline and
  while overdue.

## Projects

Project managers and officers record projects with objectives, location,
dates, department, manager, planned budget and progress. Inside a project:
milestones and deliverables, risks/challenges/delays with corrective actions,
contractors, consultants and contacts, team members, tasks, documents and
evidence, and the history of changes. **Print report** produces a project
progress report. Projects are archived, never lost.

## Documents

- **Register a document**: title, type, department, project, owner,
  confidentiality level, dates and the file (PDF, Word, Excel, images, …).
  A reference number is assigned automatically.
- **Confidentiality**: *Public* and *Internal* documents are visible to all
  users with document access. *Confidential* documents are visible only to
  the owner and authorised officers. *Restricted* documents additionally
  require an explicit grant from the owner.
- **Versions**: upload a new version with a summary of the change; all
  previous versions remain downloadable.
- **Approval and publication**: submit for approval → an authorised approver
  approves or rejects → publish. You cannot approve your own document.
- Review and expiry dates trigger reminders. Every view and download is
  logged.

## Correspondence

Document officers register **incoming** and **outgoing** letters; each gets
an official reference number (e.g. `NDC/IN/2026/0012`). Attach the scanned
letter, assign it to an officer for action with a response deadline, forward
it, record the response (linking the outgoing reply) and close it. The
history of every action is shown on the letter. Confidential letters are only
visible to authorised officers and the people handling them. Registers can be
filtered and printed.

## Meetings and calendar

Schedule meetings, appointments and official events with participants (staff
and external guests). Invited staff are notified and reminded the day
before. Prepare the agenda, attach documents, then record minutes, decisions
and action points with responsible persons and deadlines. Assignees update
the status of their action points. The **calendar** shows meetings and
action-point deadlines by month; **Print minutes** produces formal minutes.

## Assets

Every computer, printer, item of furniture or other equipment has an asset
tag. Asset officers register assets, assign them to staff or departments,
record returns and transfers, schedule and complete maintenance, and request
disposal or retirement (approved by an authorised officer). All staff can see
the asset register; the history of each asset is kept.

## Procurement and suppliers

1. **Purchase request**: list the items, quantities and estimated costs, add
   justification and attachments, and **submit** for approval.
2. An authorised approver (never the requester) approves or rejects it.
3. Procurement officers record supplier **quotations**, select one and issue a
   **purchase order**.
4. On delivery, record **goods received** per item; the request shows what is
   still outstanding.

The system never pays suppliers or approves anything automatically. Supplier
records include contacts and performance evaluations.

## Budgets and expenses

Finance officers register budgets per project, department or funding source,
with planned amounts per expense category, and submit them for approval.
Expenses are recorded against approved budgets with invoices or receipts
attached and are approved by someone other than the person who recorded
them. Budget pages compare planned and actual spending and show the remaining
balance; a warning appears when spending would exceed the budget.

## Reports

*Reports* lists the reports available to your role (projects, milestones,
tasks, documents, correspondence, meeting actions, assets, procurement,
budgets, pending/overdue items, user activity). Choose filters, then print, or
download as **PDF** or **Excel (CSV)** if you have export permission. Reports
show only records you are allowed to see.

## Approvals

*Approvals* lists everything awaiting your decision (documents, purchase
requests, budgets, expenses, asset disposals, task reviews), your own pending
requests and your recent decisions. A comment is required when rejecting.

## Good practice

- Sign out on shared computers; never share your password.
- Upload only official documents; check the confidentiality level before
  saving.
- Keep tasks and action points up to date so dashboards and reports stay
  accurate.
- Report suspicious activity or errors to the System Administrator.
